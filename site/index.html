What it is

A self-keeping organ that finds external money.

Grants exist — foundation, government, and international funding for missions like ours. They are scattered across dozens of portals, buried in eligibility fine print, and governed by deadlines that are easy to miss.

quaestor watches that landscape continuously: it discovers opportunities, checks whether we actually fit, drafts the application, and surfaces it — ready for a person to review and submit. It is the outside-money counterpart to the fleet’s internal product-revenue work; it does not duplicate it.

The pipeline

Discover → match → draft → review → submit → track.

Every opportunity walks the same path. A human stands at the gate before anything leaves.

The hard line

The organ drafts. A human sends.

Submitting is human hand

Every application is reviewed and submitted by a person. There is no auto-submit, no exceptions.

Money is human hand

quaestor never touches funds, accounts, or payment rails. It finds and drafts; people and the proper rails handle money.

Nothing here is financial, tax, or legal advice — every eligibility read is confirmed with a nonprofit attorney or grant professional.

Status

In formation.

The vision, the pipeline plans, and the governance are stood up. No grant systems have been queried and no accounts created — those are deliberate, seeded next steps. The machine is declared, not yet running.
